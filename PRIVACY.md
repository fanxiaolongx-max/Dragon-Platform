# Privacy Policy for Dragon Claw

**Last updated: November 2026**

Dragon Claw ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Dragon Claw mobile application and platform.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** Dragon Claw does not collect, sell, rent, or trade your personal information, contact lists, location data, or device identifiers to third parties.
- **Private Server Architecture:** Dragon Claw operates as a client tool that connects directly to your own configured backend server or private workspace. All conversations, execution logs, API credentials, and tool configurations are stored locally on your device or within your private self-hosted infrastructure.
- **No Third-Party Tracking / Advertising:** The App does not contain third-party tracking SDKs, advertising frameworks, or behavior analytics trackers.

---

### 2. Permissions Used
- **Network / Internet Access:** Required to communicate with your designated backend server and execute developer tools.
- **Microphone (Optional):** Used strictly in real-time voice call mode to stream audio input to your AI agents when initiated by you. Audio data is not recorded or shared with unauthorized third parties.
- **Photos / Camera (Optional):** Used only when you choose to attach images or screenshots to chats or tools.

---

### 3. Data Security
We implement strict end-to-end security measures. All network communication between the client and your backend server utilizes encrypted HTTPS / WSS protocols.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
Dragon Claw is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
